**Description** [4000]
Rotation Ready shows your volleyball lineup in all six rotations, so you and your players know exactly where to stand.

CHOOSE YOUR SYSTEM
Switch between 5-1, 5-2, 6-2 and 4-2. Each system keeps its own lineup. In a 5-2, two setters share one setter spot and you choose the rotations each one covers.

WALK THROUGH THE RALLY
Step through base positions, serve receive or the serve, the attack or switch, and defense. Arrows show who moves where, and the play button animates the whole sequence.

RUN THE MATCH
Turn on Match mode and tap +1 for each point. Rotation Ready keeps score, rotates your team when you win the serve back, shows who's serving, counts your subs against the set limit, and tells you when the libero goes in or out. Undo any point with one tap.

LOOK BACK AT EVERY MATCH
Every point in Match mode is saved with the rotation and the players on the court. See which rotations win and lose points, side-out and serving percentages, each player's plus/minus, and scoring runs for both teams, for one match, one opponent or a whole tournament, with your tournament record and results. Export a spreadsheet or copy a summary to share with your staff.

CATCH OVERLAPS
Drag any player to try your own formation. Rotation Ready checks rotational order the moment you let go and names the players who would be called for overlap.

BUILD YOUR ROSTER
Add jersey numbers (1-99), names, a libero and a seven-player bench. Use Sub to swap a bench player in, and they take over that spot in the rotation.

MAKE IT YOUR TEAM'S
Add your team name, team colors and logo. Choose a wood floor, a classic court, or a court painted in your team colors.

MADE FOR THE GYM

- Your lineups stay on your device
- No accounts, ads or tracking
