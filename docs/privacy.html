Rotation Ready

Privacy policy

Effective November 10, 2026

Rotation Ready does not collect, transmit or sell any personal information.

What the app stores

The lineups, player names, jersey numbers and positions you enter are saved only on your own device so they are there next time you open the app. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this information.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising
  • No location, contacts, camera or microphone access
  • No network connection is needed to use the app

Children

Coaches often enter the names of youth players. That information stays on the coach's device and is never collected by us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open a support request.
