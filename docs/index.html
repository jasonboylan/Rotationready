Rotation Ready
Volleyball rotations & lineups

See every rotation before the first serve.

Rotation Ready shows your lineup in all six rotations for 5-1, 6-2, 4-2 and 6-6 systems. Step through base positions, serve receive, attack and defense, drag players to try your own formations, and get an instant warning when a formation would be called for overlap.

Built for the gym. It works with no internet connection, saves your lineups on your device, and has no accounts, ads or tracking.

Features

  • 5-1, 6-2, 4-2 and 6-6 systems, receiving and serving
  • Four steps per rally with movement arrows and a play button
  • Overlap checker that names the players out of position
  • Match mode: keep score, auto-rotate on side-outs, count subs and get libero reminders
  • Jersey numbers, names, a libero and a six-player bench with quick subs
  • Your team name, colors and logo, on a wood floor or a court in team colors
  • Built for iPhone and iPad
