Support

Need help or found a problem? Send a support request and describe what happened, which device you were using, and the system and rotation you were viewing.

Common questions

Where are my lineups saved?
On your device only. They do not sync between your iPhone and iPad.
How do I sub a player in?
Enter the bench player in the Bench section, then use Sub to choose who comes out. The sub takes over that player's spot in the rotation.
Why is a player outlined in red?
At the moment of the serve, that player is out of rotational order with a neighbor. The message under the court says which two players overlap.
How do I add my team colors and logo?
Tap the logo at the top of the app, or scroll to Team colors & logo. Pick your colors, choose an image for the logo, and choose a court surface. Restore default look puts everything back.
How does Match mode work?
Tap Match mode under the court, choose who serves first, then tap +1 for each point. When your team wins the serve back, the app rotates you and switches to serving. Subs made with the Sub button are counted automatically, and you can adjust the count with − and +. Undo takes back the last point.
How do I undo my changes?
Use Reset this step under the court to undo moves on one step, or Reset lineup, numbers and moves at the bottom of the lineup panel to start over for that system.
